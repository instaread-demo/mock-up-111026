Can You Return A Rental Car Early?

AirlineAirport » Blog » Can You Return A Rental Car Early?

Yes, in most cases, you can return a rental car early. However, returning a rental car early may affect the rental price, refund, prepaid amount, or other charges. Exact rules vary by the rental company, booking type, rental rate, location, and payment terms. Some rental companies recalculate the price based on the days the vehicle was used, while others may keep the original rate or charge an early-return fee. Prepaid and promotional bookings can have different rules, and an early return may not qualify for a refund.

What Happens if You Return A Rental Car Early?

When you return a rental car before the due date, the rental company will close the rental agreement and recalculate the final price based on their pricing rules. Rental companies often use different pricing structures for one-day, multi-day, weekly, and promotional rentals. 

Short-Term Rental: You can return a rental car early without reducing the rental cost when you book a short rental period. 

Weekend Rental: If you get a special weekend rate, returning the car early may cancel the discount and change your total rental cost. 

Discount Offer: If you receive a discounted rate for keeping the car for three or more days, returning it early may increase your daily rate or remove the discount. 

Weekly Rentals: If you book a weekly rate, returning a rental car early may not change the price. However, returning it much earlier could cancel the weekly rate and result in higher daily charges. 

Tip: Contact the rental company before returning the car to avoid any confusion about charges, return location, and hours.

Can You Return a Rental Car Early After Business Hours? 

Some branches offer after-hours key drop-off services, while others require the vehicle to be returned during business hours. Therefore, before planning an unattended return, check the branch’s instructions:

  • Ask where to park
  • How to lock the car 
  • Where to leave the keys 
  • When the rental is officially checked in.

When possible, return the car during staffed hours. If you use an approved key drop, take photos of the parked vehicle and keep any return confirmation. Never leave the keys somewhere the branch has not approved.

How to Return a Rental Car Early?

You can return the rental car early smoothly by following the steps below:

  • Inform the rental company about your early return through its official support channels and ask about the revised rental cost, any fees, and extra charges.
  • Collect the original confirmation, rental agreement, and payment receipt.
  • Remove all personal belongings, check the fuel policy, and check for any damage.
  • Ask for a return receipt or confirmation showing the actual return date and time. 

When Can You Return a Rental Car Early?

You might return a rental car for several reasons. Such as:

  • Your trip ends earlier than planned.
  • Public transportation, hotel shuttles, or trains are enough for the rest of the trip.
  • An earlier return flight can make the original rental period unnecessary. 
  • A change in itinerary or accommodation, or even a cancelled plan, may require you to return the rental car early.
  • Unexpected personal circumstances or medical emergencies may require an early return. 

Will You Get a Refund for Unused Days?

You can return a rental car early, but whether you will get a refund depends on your booking terms.

Prepaid Reservations: A prepaid rental may still be charged in full even if you return the car several days early. However, the rules can vary by rental company and booking channel. Before returning the car early, ask if you can get a refund or credit. 

Charges According to Use: Some rental agreements may use the actual number of days rented to determine the final cost. Ask for a revised total rather than estimating the refund by dividing your original payment by the number of booked days.

Rental Car Early Return Policy

The early rental car return policies can vary by rental company. Check the specific reservation terms before returning early. 

Avis: The supplier usually does not provide a refund if you return a prepaid rental before your original return date. If you shorten your rental period, your rate may also change, so your original prepaid daily rate may no longer apply. 

Enterprise: The company charges based on the actual length of the rental for early return. The final rate can change when a shorter rental period applies. The exact amount for Enterprise rental car early return depends on the original booking and rental terms. 

Dollar: You generally pay only for the time you keep the car on a pay-later rental. However, returning it more than 24 hours early without enough notice may result in an early-return fee. Pay-now bookings generally do not offer refunds for unused rental days.  

Hertz: You can return the rental car early, but the financial result depends on the rental terms. Under certain US terms, a non-prepaid rental returned more than 24 hours early can be subject to an early-return fee, while the rental cost may also be recalculated. Some prepaid rates have separate rules for unused days.

AVIS/Budget: Avis and Budget rental car early return policy states that return prices are adjusted based on the length of the original reservation. Prepaid and promotional bookings can have different rules and may not qualify for a refund for unused days.

Sixt: Returning a car early can affect the rental price, as the original booking may have been based on a specific rental period. Prepaid and promotional rates may have separate refund restrictions. 

National: National car rental early return policy can vary by country and location. In some published locations, no refund is provided for returning the vehicle early. The final charge may therefore depend on the original rate and the specific rental agreement. 

Payless: A shorter rental period may affect the originally quoted rate, and prepaid reservations may have limited or no refunds for unused days. 

Thrifty: You can return a rental car early, but the term depends on the payment type. Pay-later generally allows you to pay for the time you use the vehicle, while pay-now bookings do not receive refunds for unused days. A $25 early-return fee can apply to certain non-prepaid rentals returned more than 24 hours early without advance notice.

When Does Early Return Cost More?

Returning a rental car early does not always lower the total rental cost. The final cost may increase in the following cases: 

Weekly or Monthly Rates: Returning early may remove a discounted weekly or monthly rate and increase the daily rate. 

Prepaid Bookings: Prepaid rates may have different rules, and unused rental days may not be refundable. 

Promotional Rates: Special offers may apply for a minimum rental period. Ending the rental early could make the discount invalid. 

Changes to taxes and fees: Some location-based charges or taxes may change if you return a rental car early. 

Optional Services: Items such as GPS, child seats, or protection products may have minimum charges or separate terms that affect the final bill. 

Price Recalculation: The company may recalculate the rental using the rate that applies to the shorter rental period. 

When Does Early Return Help to Save Money?

Early return may help save money in these situations: 

Daily Bookings: A daily rental with no special weekly or monthly discount may cost less when you return the rental car early. 

Shortening a Weekly Rental: A weekly rate may be only slightly higher than a five-day rental cost.

No Minimum Rental Period: Rentals without minimum-day requirements are more likely to offer savings on early return.

Pay-as-you-go Rentals: Non-prepaid rentals may be charged according to the actual rental period, so returning early can reduce the number of billable days. 

No Early-Return Fee: If the supplier does not charge a fee for returning early, ending the rental sooner can lower the total cost. 

Can You Return A Rental Car Early at a Different Location?

Usually, returning early to the original agreed location is the simplest option. However, returning a rental car early at a different location can be treated differently. 

Some companies may apply one-way rental fees, depending on the locations and booking terms. Before dropping the vehicle at another location, get approval from the rental company. Returning it somewhere else without permission could result in additional charges. 

How to Avoid Problems When Returning a Rental Car Early?

Follow the tips below to avoid problems before ending your rental early:

  • Check the rental agreement if you can return the rental car early.
  • Contact the rental company to learn about the policies and after-hours return rules.
  • Check whether the rental was prepaid or an early-return fee applies. 
  • Ask whether the daily or weekly rate will change.
  • Follow the correct return-location instructions. 
  • Inspect the car for damage and take photos before returning it.
  • Keep all booking and payment records until the transaction is complete.

End Notes!

You can return a rental car early, but the final rental cost depends on the rental company’s policy, the type of reservation, whether the booking was prepaid, the original rental rate, and any applicable fees. Some companies may recalculate the rental based on the actual number of days used, while others may not refund unused days or may apply an early-return charge. It’s better to check the rental terms, refunds, or additional fees, and contact the supplier before returning the vehicle to avoid any issues later. 

Frequently Asked Question (FAQs)

Can I return a rental car early?

Yes, most rental companies allow early return, but the final price depends on the rental agreement and booking type.

Will you get a refund for returning the rental car early?

Some rentals may be recalculated, while prepaid or promotional bookings may not provide a refund for unused rental days.

Can returning a rental car early increase final cost?

A shorter rental period may replace a discounted weekly or promotional rate with a higher applicable daily rate. Some companies may also apply an early-return fee.

Should I tell the rental company before returning a car early?

Yes, informing the supplier helps confirm the updated return time, pricing, and any possible fees.

What happens if you return a prepaid rental car early?

Usually, the unused portion may not be refundable.

Can I return a rental car early to a different location?

Yes, but the rental company may treat changing the return location as a one-way rental change, which can result in an additional charge.

Will I still pay for insurance if I return the car early?

The final cost for insurance and optional protection products varies by rental company and location.
