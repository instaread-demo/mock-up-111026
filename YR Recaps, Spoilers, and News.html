Daniel and Traci receive job offers from Devon, but they need time to decide. Abby has a medical emergency as Society. Victor drops by Abbott Mansion to ask Jack to help keep Adam and Billy away from each other. Nick gets caught in the middle of an argument between Sharon and Nikki and then admits to Sharon he’s been keeping a stash of drugs to test his resolve. Nikki and Diane fight over the GCAC Jazz Lounge for Phyllis’s entertainment, and when Daniel expresses his disappointment in how Phyllis is handling their request she leaves the decision to him. This episode of The Young and the Restless airs Tuesday, November 10th, 2026 in the USA, and one day ahead in Canada.

Society – Abby Says She’s Feeling Better and Devon Meets With Traci to Make Her an Offer

Abby spots Devon working away on his laptop at the bar and kisses him hello. She dropped Dominic off for a school trip this morning.

Devon asks how Abby is feeling, specifically about her dizziness and nausea. She says she’s feeling fine and suggests that maybe she just had a bug that she’s since gotten over.

Devon tries to talk her into seeing a doctor anyway, but she insists that he drop it.

Traci arrives to meet with Devon. They grab a table and Abby brings out some food for Traci.

Traci wonders what this is all about and assures them they don’t need to butter her up if they need her to babysit Dominic. Devon says he’ll hold her to that, but that’s not why he’s asked to meet with her. Abby leaves and Devon explains he wants to make Traci an offer.

Crimson Lights – Nikki and Sharon Clash Over the Jabot Ad Once Again With Nick Caught in the Middle, Then Nick Admits to Sharon That He Never Got Rid of Joe’s Drugs and Kept Them to Test His Resolve

Nikki approaches the counter and curtly asks Sharon for a tea, “to go.” It’s clear that she’s still upset with her.

Sharon can’t believe the attitude and asks if she’s seriously going to hold a grudge over the photo shoot. Nikki says she is: that photo shoot was for an ad campaign for a company in direct competition with hers!

Sharon objects that Vibrante hasn’t even launched and it never crossed her mind that she would object. Nikki says Sharon should always check with the Newmans before agreeing to do anything with Jabot. Sharon can’t believe what she’s hearing and demands to know why she would do that. Nikki says it’s a conflict of interest.

Sharon thinks it’s just about Victor’s hatred of Jack and the fact that Nikki is threatened by Diane. Nikki denies it as Nick walks through the door and catches them mid-argument. He demands to know what he’s just walked in on.

Nikki asks Nick if he’s aware that his ex-wife is a traitor. Nick wants to know what Nikki means by that. Sharon explains about the photo shoot for Jabot and says she was honored to be included. Nick thinks that sounds great and asks why Nikki would have a problem with that.

Sharon talks over Nikki to explain that she didn’t take into consideration that Nikki is running Vibrante and apparently now she’s committed a cardinal sin. It’s apparent that she doesn’t take Nikki’s concerns very seriously.

Nick tells Nikki it’s no big deal, but Nikki complains that Diane purposefully planned this ad campaign to coincide with the launch of Vibrante. She considers it a direct attack.

Nick can understand why Nikki would be upset, but Sharon says she didn’t set out to attack Nikki; she was just asked to do something and it sounded like a good idea. Nick understands her perspective, too.

Nick suggests both of them just step back and chill out, telling Nikki that clearly Sharon didn’t go out of her way to try and “get” Nikki. Nikki says she should have expected Nick to take Sharon’s side. Nick denies he’s taking sides.

Nikki says this is all part of Sharon’s pattern of making questionable decisions. She cancels her tea order and storms out of the coffee shop. Nick and Sharon are stunned into silence for a moment.

Once they’ve had a moment to process, Sharon explains that Nikki showed up at the photo shoot and attacked everyone there, including Lily, whom she believed was betraying Victor after he “gifted” Chancellor to her.

Nick says Nikki is pouring her heart and soul into Vibrante and it’s a lot of pressure. Sharon thinks Nick is making excuses for Nikki. She’s upset that Nikki ruined what was going to be a good experience for her. She adds that if Diane asked, she’d do it again.

Eventually Sharon apologizes for making it all about her since Nick walked in, and asks how he’s doing. Nick admits he’s kind of struggling and he’s come to ask for a favor.

Nick admits that he went to see Marie and it was harder than he expected. Sharon asks what he means.

Nick pulls a handful of pills out of his pocket and drops them on the table.

Sharon is horrified and asks where Nick got the drugs. Nick admits he got them from Joe after knocking him out, and he’s kept them ever since. He admits he told Adam he threw them away, but he kept them to test himself. Sharon asks if he passed his test.

Abbott Mansion – Victor Admits to Jack That Adam Has Developed an Unhealthy Obsession With Ruby and Asks for His Help Keeping Adam and Billy Apart

Jack wants Diane to join him for breakfast, but she’s on her way out the door; she has an errand to run before work.

Diane kisses Jack and runs off, and the doorbell rings. Jack answers to find Victor standing there.

Victor says he’s here to talk about Billy. Jack invites him in, suggesting that Victor must have come to share his good wishes for Billy’s baby, new marriage, or recovering mother. Victor warns that Billy is about to cause big problems for the Newmans and says that Jack needs to stop him.

Jack doesn’t think there’s any stopping Billy. Victor explains that Billy visited Adam’s house on the ranch, and he thinks his plan was to cause trouble. Victor asks Jack for his help in making sure Billy and Adam keep their distance from one another.

Jack knows that if Victor’s come to him he must be very concerned. He asks if it’s about Adam and Ruby. Victor confirms it. He worries that Adam wants to visit Ruby too much and is defensive about it when confronted. He adds that Adam is keeping Chelsea in the dark about his frequent visits to see Ruby.

Jack promises to keep an eye on things and Victor says he appreciates it and wishes him a nice day. He shows himself out.

Genoa City Athletic Club – Daniel Loses Patience With Phyllis’s Thirst for Revenge, Then Nikki and Diane Fight Over the Jazz Lounge

Daniel is sitting at a table with Phyllis, discussing business. Daniel wants to make use of some of Arabesque’s older properties that he considers viable, but Phyllis is only interested in new acquisitions: specifically, she wants to scoop up properties that are of interest to Victor.

Daniel doesn’t like the idea of an entire business model based on eating Victor’s lunch. Phyllis argues that Victor has already vetted all the properties that she wants to acquire, and if they’re good enough for Victor they’re good enough for them.

Daniel admits he’s new to this sort of business but says he thinks this plan sucks. Phyllis insists that she knows what she’s doing.

Daniel says he’s done and stands to leave. He tells Phyllis that her next hire should be a CRO: a Chief Revenge Officer. He walks away.

Diane enters and says Phyllis is just the person she wanted to see. Phyllis offers to get the hostess to seat her. Diane says she’s about to make Phyllis’s day.

Phyllis is primed to say no to whatever Diane wants, but Diane insists she hear her out: she wants to book the Jazz Lounge for Jabot for Valentine’s Day. Phyllis asks why that would make her day.

Diane says Phyllis can name her price. Phyllis says Valentine’s Day is a very big day for the Athletic Club. Diane offers to book some rooms as well as the lounge. She says Jack wants this too, and Phyllis asks why Jack would send the “B-team”, noting that she and Diane don’t like each other.

Diane says it doesn’t have to be personal; she just wants to make a deal. Phyllis says it’s a big ask and she’ll have to think about it. Diane says she’ll pay more for an immediate response.

Phyllis wonders why Diane is so desperate to book the lounge, and Nikki walks in. Nikki explains that she has her own plans for the lounge, and that’s why Diane is sniffing around. She thinks Diane is trying to sabotage her plans.

Diane thinks this is all about yesterday’s photo shoot, and Phyllis wants details. Diane would rather go talk business with Phyllis privately, but Nikki thinks they should fill Phyllis in on what happened at the photo shoot.

Phyllis refuses to leave and demands to be told all about it. Nikki explains about the Jabot ad campaign. Diane says Nikki is scared because all she has is a name and logo.

They go back and forth while Phyllis enjoys the show. Nikki reveals that she’s got Pietro on board and asks if he’s the one who revealed her plans. Diane denies it.

Diane asks Phyllis if they have a deal, and Nikki warns Phyllis she’d better not take Diane’s offer.

Phyllis offers to let them both plead their case then she’ll take some time and decide who gets to rent the lounge. Diane says she asked first so she should get it; Nikki says she’ll double Diane’s offer.

Diane says it’ll be more prestigious for the Athletic Club to host a Jabot event. The two of them argue for Phyllis’s enjoyment, and she stirs the pot because she doesn’t want the fun to end. She says she’s not impressed with either of their arguments and asks for more.

Nikki says that Phyllis should show her gratitude to Victor for gifting her Arabesque by doing business with Vibrante. Diane argues that Victor wanted Phyllis to go to prison, and Diane owes the Newmans nothing.

Society – Devon Offers Jobs to Both Traci and Daniel, Who Both Need Time to Think, Then Abby Has Some Kind of Medical Emergency

Daniel comes in to sit at the bar and Abby asks why he’s looking to day drink. Daniel says there’s only one reason: Phyllis. Abby is not surprised that she’s causing trouble, but is surprised that Daniel is talking to her again.

Abby lends an ear, and Daniel explains that he started working at Arabesque with Phyllis, and it’s more complicated than he expected. He loves Phyllis, but he can’t deal with her paranoia and aggressiveness. He’s more interested in working with people than grinding them into the ground.

Abby thinks it makes sense that they’re butting heads. Daniel wonders if he’s made the wrong choice joining Arabesque. Abby admits she never thought of this as something Daniel would want to do. Daniel asks what she thinks he should do.

Back at the table, Devon explains that he’d like Traci to head up the creative writing department at his new college.

Traci is flattered but she’s on a tight writing deadline. Devon says she has time; enrollments don’t start until next fall. Traci wants time to think it over, so she asks to switch tables and process this alone. Devon says that’s fine. Traci says that no matter what she decides, she’s sure his college will be magnificent.

Abby brings Daniel over and asks if he’s still in “hiring mode.” She suggests Daniel become his new Chair of Graphic Arts at the Neil Winters College of Arts and Humanities. This is news to Daniel; Abby didn’t warn him what her plan was. Devon admits it was actually his idea.

Daniel is surprised that Devon wants to hire him after his failed relationship with Lily. Devon says he knows that Daniel and Lily have moved on and he’s sorry Daniel lost Heather.

Daniel says he’s not not interested, but he just started working at Arabesque with Phyllis. Devon says if Daniel is happy working with his mom he’s happy for him, but if he’d rather do something more creative he can come work for him.

Daniel promises to be in touch and thanks them for keeping him in mind. He leaves.

Devon says he’s got two “firm maybes,” and he and Abby kiss.

Abby goes to chat with Traci. Abby says that ever since he began working on the college, she feels like she has her husband back. Traci asks how Abby is and she admits that she recently thought she might be pregnant but wound up disappointed. Traci agrees with Devon, saying Abby should see a doctor. Traci says that’s an order, collects her things, and leaves.

Abby goes to talk to Devon and begins feeling strange. She’s having trouble breathing and her heartbeat is getting very fast. Devon asks someone to call an ambulance while he tries to calm her down.

Genoa City Athletic Club – Daniel Returns to Find Phyllis Torturing Diane and Nikki for Amusement and Disapproves, So Phyllis Leaves the Decision Is His Hands

Daniel returns while Nikki and Diane are petitioning Phyllis for the use of the Jazz Lounge and asks what’s going on. Phyllis explains the situation, and both Diane and Nikki demand a decision.

Daniel is disappointed that Phyllis is sitting here humiliating Nikki and Diane when they’ve come to make a deal in good faith.

Phyllis pulls Daniel aside and asks what’s wrong with him. Daniel says she’s using the company as a stick to beat her perceived enemies. Phyllis offers to let Daniel make the choice. Speaking loud enough for Nikki and Diane to hear, she paints each of their proposals in a very negative light for Daniel, offending them, and then walks away.
