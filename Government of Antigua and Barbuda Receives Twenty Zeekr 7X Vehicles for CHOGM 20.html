Purposes & Features

Consent

Cookies, device or similar online identifiers (e.g. login-based identifiers, randomly assigned identifiers, network based identifiers) together with other information (e.g. browser type and information, language, screen size, supported technologies etc.) can be stored or read on your device to recognise it each time it connects to an app or to a website, for one or several of the purposes presented here.

Illustrations

  • Most purposes explained in this notice rely on the storage or accessing of information from your device when you use an app or visit a website. For example, a vendor or publisher might need to store a cookie on your device during your first visit on a website, to be able to recognise your device during your next visits (by accessing this cookie each time).

Number of Vendors seeking consent: 859

Legitimate Interest
Consent

Advertising presented to you on this service can be based on limited data, such as the website or app you are using, your non-precise location, your device type or which content you are (or have been) interacting with (for example, to limit the number of times an ad is presented to you).

Illustrations

  • A car manufacturer wants to promote its electric vehicles to environmentally conscious users living in the city after office hours. The advertising is presented on a page with related content (such as an article on climate change actions) after 6:30 p.m. to users whose non-precise location suggests that they are in an urban zone.
  • A large producer of watercolour paints wants to carry out an online advertising campaign for its latest watercolour range, diversifying its audience to reach as many amateur and professional artists as possible and avoiding showing the ad next to mismatched content (for instance, articles about how to paint your house). The number of times that the ad has been presented to you is detected and limited, to avoid presenting it too often.

Number of Vendors seeking consent or relying on legitimate interest: 803

Consent

Information about your activity on this service (such as forms you submit, content you look at) can be stored and combined with other information about you (for example, information from your previous activity on this service and other websites or apps) or similar users. This is then used to build or improve a profile about you (that might include possible interests and personal aspects). Your profile can be used (also later) to present advertising that appears more relevant based on your possible interests by this and other entities.

Illustrations

Government of Antigua and Barbuda Receives Twenty Zeekr 7X Vehicles for CHOGM 2026

St. John's, Antigua and Barbuda--(Newsfile Corp. - November 9, 2026) - The Government of Antigua and Barbuda has formally received twenty (20) Zeekr 7X electric vehicles from Geely Auto Mexico for use as official vehicles during the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM) 2026. The vehicles, provided under a sponsorship arrangement, arrived at St. John's Harbour on 1 November 2026, with the formal handover taking place on 2 November at Camp Blizzard.

Present were the Honourable E.P. Chet Greene, Minister of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Immigration and Chair of the CHOGM 2026 Taskforce; Her Excellency Jiang Wei, Ambassador of the People's Republic of China to Antigua and Barbuda; and Ms Huanke Bao (Echo), Marketing Representative of Geely Motors Mexico.

Minister Greene expressed the Government's appreciation for the sponsorship and its contribution to CHOGM 2026 logistics.

"On behalf of the Prime Minister, the Right Honourable Gaston Browne, and the people of Antigua and Barbuda, I wish to thank Geely Motors for providing these transport solutions in support of CHOGM 2026," Minister Greene said. "It is also my hope that the Geely and Zeekr brands will find favour with the Antiguan and Barbudan public and remain a presence on our roads long after CHOGM."

The Minister also expressed heartfelt gratitude to the Embassy of the People's Republic of China for its support in facilitating the partnership.

Her Excellency Ambassador Jiang Wei stated that China was honoured to support Antigua and Barbuda's hosting of CHOGM, following its assistance for SIDS4 and the General Assembly of the Organization of American States. "We hope that this fleet provided by Geely will make a tangible contribution to Antigua and Barbuda's successful hosting of the upcoming Commonwealth Meeting," she said.

Bryan Wu, CEO of Geely Auto Mexico thanked the Government for its trust, support and partnership, and expressed the company's hope that the initiative would lead to further opportunities to expand electric-vehicle options in Antigua and Barbuda.

The initiative follows Minister Greene's mandate for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Trade, and Immigration to pursue sustainable transport solutions for CHOGM 2026. The Embassy of Antigua and Barbuda to the United Arab Emirates, through His Excellency Theon Ali, Deputy Head of Mission and Ambassador to Qatar and Syria, coordinated with the Embassy of the People's Republic of China in St. John's and the Ministry's Headquarters to advance the partnership.

The twenty electric vehicles strengthen the Government's VVIP/VIP transport capacity for CHOGM 2026 while supporting Antigua and Barbuda's broader interest in sustainable, lower-emission transportation.

About ZEEKR

Zeekr is a global luxury technology brand under Geely Auto Group, officially launched in April 2021. Capitalizing on Geely Auto Group's deep industrial foundation and global resource advantages, Zeekr rapidly built a comprehensive intelligent technology ecosystem. To date, it has rolled out eight models encompassing four core product matrices. Since deliveries began in October 2021, Zeekr has accumulated over 900,000 vehicle deliveries. Zeekr has now entered over 50 countries and regions, operating more than 650 stores worldwide.
