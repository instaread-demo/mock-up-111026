Lavender and Grapefruit Buck

I’m a particular fan of the “buck” style drinks, mostly because they are lighter and usually have some sort of ginger beer in them. This Lavender and Grapefruit Buck does just that. It’s an easy cocktail to make, and honestly, is really delicious without being too heavy on the alcohol content.

You can, if you like, use an alcoholic ginger beer, but there’s no need here. This gin cocktail is simply gin, with dozens of flavor options. London dry gin is the best style for this cocktail, and there are loads of great ones. You’ll also need grapefruit juice, lavender syrup, and your pick of ginger beer. Reed’s is a great ginger beer that’s easy to find.

Lavender and Grapefruit Buck Gin Cockatil

This delightful and fresh gin cocktail pairs ginger beer, grapefruit, and lavender syrup with good ol' gin.

Ingredients

  • .5 Oz. Lavender cocktail syrup
  • 1.5 Oz. Grapefruit juice
  • 1.5 Oz. Gin
  • 1.5 Oz. Ginger beer

Instructions

  • Build this drink in your glass!
  • Fill a collins glass, or pint glass, with ice.
  • Add your gin, lavender syrup, and grapefruit juice.
  • Stir with your cocktail bar spoon.
  • Top with your choice of ginger beer and give one last swish around with your barspoon.

If you need to make your own lavender syrup, here’s the recipe for that. You can also buy some lavender syrup and have it land on your doorstep!

Fresh Lavender Syrup for Cocktails

Easily make some fresh Lavender Cocktail Syrup with sugar, water, and fresh lavender blossoms.

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp. Fresh lavender blossoms
  • 1 Cup Sugar
  • 1 Cup Water
  • 2-5 Whole Blueberries

Instructions

  • Start by "plucking" the tiny blossoms from fresh lavender flowers. Collect 1 tablespoon's worth.
  • Combine your blossoms, water, and sugar into a saucepan or small pot.
  • Add some whole blueberries if you want a deeper color to your finished syrup.
  • Bring all of this to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
  • Simmer for 10 minutes, then remove from heat and cool thoroughly.
  • Strain and funnel into a glass container.
  • Store in the fridge.

Ideas for the Lavender and Grapefruit Buck

Honestly, I don’t have any. This drink is just fine as is. I suppose you could up the gin, lighten up on the gin, or any other flavors.

There’s also a similar drink, the Grapefruit and Lavender Martini, which is also quite delightful. No punch of ginger beer, though.

Cheers, and make good decisions!
