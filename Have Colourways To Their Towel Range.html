Loop Home Adds Two Must-Have Colourways To Their Towel Collection

Shop Loop Home’s full collection of new organic cotton bath sheets, towels, and mats.

Loop Home has introduced two new colourways to its organic cotton bath towel range, bringing calming tones and architectural lines to your bathroom.

Sky & Carmine pairs soft blue with rich burgundy, while Midnight & Sand combines deep navy with a warm, earthy beige. Available as bath sheets, bath towels, hand towels, and bath mats, each pairing brings a touch of colour and finesse to any space.

Made with cotton grown in Türkiye’s Aegean region and woven at a family-run mill in Denizli, these towels are free of harmful chemicals and feel soft on the skin.

Both new colourways are available in three classic Loop Home patterns: Sky & Carmine in Brick, Dual Dash, and Pinstripe, and Midnight & Sand Stack, Dual Dash, and Tile.

And, to make life easier, Loop Home is offering bundles that pair two bath towels or two bath sheets, plus two hand towels and one bath mat, saving 15% off individual prices.

Whether you’re updating your bath linens or getting a start on Christmas shopping, don’t miss the chance to explore this full range of bath sheets, towels, and mats.
